XI Board is an iPhone app for building soccer formations, saving them by team and match, and sharing them as images. Find answers to common questions and contact the developer below.

FEATURES

Key features

  • 41 formation presetsClassic 3-back, 4-back and 5-back formations for 11-a-side, plus formations for 8-a-side, 7-a-side, 5-a-side and futsal. Alignment guides help you line up players as you drag.
  • Drag-and-drop substitutionsLong-press a starter, substitute or unselected player and drag to swap them. You can also swap two starters.
  • Teams and rostersAdd each player's number, name, photo, multiple positions and notes. Keep notes for each team, too.
  • Formations and results for each matchRecord the date, competition, round, opponent, home/away, venue and result, including penalty shoot-outs. Save several formations for one match, such as first-half and second-half lineups.
  • Captain, cards and injuriesShow a captain's armband and booked, sent-off and injury markers on players. They also appear in shared images.
  • 1st, 2nd and 3rd kitsRegister up to 3 kits each for outfield players and goalkeepers, and choose which to show for each formation.
  • Kit examples by country and seasonChoose examples from Japan, England, Spain, Italy, Germany, France, South Korea and the USA, or plain white kits with or without a collar, then customize them. Locked examples become available after you watch a video ad to the end.
  • Customize the pitchChoose Top View, Angled View or Low Angle in portrait or landscape, and change the grass pattern and color. Your choices are saved for each formation.
  • Share as an imageExport your team name, formation name, match (competition, round and opponent) and substitutes in one image, laid out just like the screen.
  • iCloud Sync and automatic backupsSync your data across devices using the same Apple ID, and restore earlier data from daily automatic backups (up to seven are kept).
  • Appearance and languageChoose Light, Dark or Match Device. Available in 10 languages, including English and Japanese.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Your teams, players, formations, matches, kits and player photos are stored on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server operated by the developer. If you turn on iCloud Sync in the Settings tab, your data is also stored in your own iCloud Drive. If you delete the app without using iCloud Sync, your data is deleted too.

Can I move my data to a new iPhone?

Yes, with iCloud Sync.

  1. On your current iPhone, open the Settings tab, turn on iCloud Sync and tap Sync Now.
  2. On your new iPhone, sign in to iCloud with the same Apple ID and install XI Board.
  3. On your new iPhone, turn on iCloud Sync in the Settings tab.

iCloud Drive must be turned on in iPhone Settings on both devices. If your new iPhone already has data, you will be asked which data to use. The data you don't use is kept as an automatic backup.

How do I go back to earlier data?

In the Settings tab, open Restore from Backup under Automatic Backup and choose the backup you want. Restoring replaces your current teams and formations with the contents of that backup. Automatic backups are created when at least a day has passed and your data has changed, and up to seven are kept. You can also create one with Back Up Now.

How do I make a substitution?

Long-press a player, drag them onto the player you want to swap with, and release. You can swap two players on the pitch, or a player on the pitch with a substitute. Unselected players appear under Members at the bottom of the formation screen; long-press one there to move them to the pitch or the substitutes. Dragging a player on the pitch down to the buttons at the bottom opens Members.

How do I save formations for a match?

Tap the Match info button on the formation screen and enter the date, competition, round, opponent and so on. When you save, the formation is added to Matches if match info is entered, or to Formations if it is left blank.

To add another formation to the same match, such as for the second half, tap + to the right of the formation name and choose Add to the same match. Switch between formations in the same match with the < and > buttons beside the formation name, or by swiping across the pitch.

How do I use 8-a-side or futsal formations?

Tap the Formations button at the bottom of the formation screen, choose 11-a-side, 8-a-side, 7-a-side, 5-a-side or futsal under Players, then choose a formation. You can set the format for each formation.

What appears in shared images?

Players on the pitch and in Substitutes appear. Players in Not Selected do not. The image also shows the team name, the formation name and, for matches, the competition, round and opponent. Match results are not shown. Images follow the app's appearance (Light or Dark).

How do I use a kit with a lock icon?

Tap the locked kit example, choose Watch Ad and watch the video ad to the end. Each kit is unlocked separately. Once unlocked, a kit stays available on that device. If you delete the app or use another device, you will need to watch an ad again. Plain kits are available without ads.

How do I use 2nd and 3rd kits?

Add 2nd and 3rd kits with the + button on the kit editing screen (goalkeepers work the same way). Choose which kit to show on the pitch for each formation with the Uniform button at the bottom of the formation screen.

How do I change the pitch angle or color?

Tap the Pitch Display button at the bottom of the formation screen to choose the angle (Top View, Angled View or Low Angle, each in portrait or landscape), grass pattern and color. Besides the preset colors, you can pick any color. These settings are saved for each formation.

How do I set a captain, cards or injuries?

Tap a player on the formation screen, set Captain or Cards & injuries in the editing screen, and tap Save. Each formation has one captain. Choosing a different captain removes the armband from the previous player. In a team's member editor, you can mark several players as captain candidates.

Can I undo or redo edits?

Use Undo on the formation screen to reverse your last edit, and Redo to restore an action you have undone. The buttons are unavailable when there is nothing to undo or redo.

How do I line up players neatly?

When you drag a player on the pitch, guide lines appear where the player aligns horizontally or vertically with another player, at the center of the pitch, or in a symmetrical position. Move close to snap into place, or keep dragging to place the player freely.

My player edits aren't applied.

When you edit a player from the formation screen, tap Save to apply your changes. If you close without saving, your changes are not applied. In the team screen's member editor, changes are saved automatically when you close the screen.

How do I change to dark mode or another language?

Choose Light, Dark or Match Device under Appearance in the Settings tab. To change the display language, tap Language in the Settings tab to open XI Board's page in the iPhone Settings app.

I see a message that my saved data couldn't be loaded.

The app keeps the original data on your device under a different name and tries to recover whatever data it can read. Do not delete the app; first check the message in the app. If you have automatic backups, you can restore one with Restore from Backup in the Settings tab. If the problem continues, please contact us.

Can I remove ads?

An option to remove ads is not currently available. Ads allow us to offer the app for free.
