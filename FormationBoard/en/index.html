Key features

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Your teams, players, formations, kits and player photos are all stored on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server operated by the developer. Deleting the app also deletes this data.

Can I move my data to a new iPhone?

Tap the gear button in the upper-right corner of the team list to open Settings, then choose Export Data under Backup to save a backup file (JSON). Player photos are included. On your new iPhone, choose Import Data on the same screen and select that file. Importing replaces the data currently on that device.

How do I make a substitution?

Long-press a player in Substitutes or Not Selected at the bottom of the formation screen, then drag them onto a player on the pitch and release. Players on the pitch can be dragged to move their position.

Substitutes don't appear in my shared image.

When the Substitutes / Not Selected area is collapsed, substitutes are left out of the shared image. Tap the Members tab above the area to expand it, then share again.

How do I use 2nd and 3rd kits?

Tap "+" in the kit area of the team screen to add a 2nd or 3rd kit (goalkeepers too). You can choose which kit to show on the pitch—1st, 2nd or 3rd—for each formation.

How do I change the pitch view?

Use the Pitch Angle button on the formation screen to choose Top View, Angled View or Low Angle, each in portrait or landscape.

Can I remove ads?

An option to remove ads is not currently available. Ads help keep the app free.
