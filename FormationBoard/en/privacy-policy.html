PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy
Policy

This policy explains how XI Board (“the App”) handles the data you create and advertising-related information.

Effective: June 27, 2026 / Updated: November 7, 2026Applies to: XI Board for iOS

1. Our approach

The XI Board developer (“the Developer”) handles only the minimum information needed to provide the App. Creating and editing formations takes place entirely on your device. No account is required to use the App.

2. Data stored on your device

The App stores the following information on your iPhone:

  • Team names, and player names, numbers, positions, captain settings and notes
  • Player photos that you choose
  • Formations, drafts, player placement, kit settings, and card and injury markers
  • Match information (date, competition, round, opponent, home/away, venue and result)
  • Display settings, such as appearance, the pitch angle, pattern and color, and whether numbers and photos are shown
  • Which sample kits you have unlocked by watching ads

The Developer does not transmit this data to a Developer-operated server and cannot access or view it. Depending on your Apple device backup settings (such as iCloud Backup), the data may be included in a device backup.

3. Access to photos

When you add a player photo, the App uses the standard iOS photo picker. The App receives only the photos you select and does not access your entire photo library. Selected photos are resized and stored on your device (and also in your own iCloud Drive if iCloud Sync is turned on).

4. Shared images

When you use the share feature, an image that may include team and formation names, match information (competition, round and opponent), player names and numbers, and player photos is sent to the destination you choose. Sharing happens only when you initiate it. Once shared, the information is handled under the terms and privacy policy of the destination service.

5. iCloud Sync and automatic backups

  • iCloud Sync (off by default): When turned on, your data, including player photos, is stored in the iCloud Drive of your own Apple ID and synced with your other devices signed in to the same Apple ID.
  • Automatic backups (on by default): When at least a day has passed since the last backup and your data has changed, a backup is created, and up to seven backups are kept. If you are signed in to iCloud, backups are saved to the “XI Board” folder in iCloud Drive; otherwise they are saved on your device.

Data stored in iCloud Drive is managed by Apple and handled under Apple's Privacy Policy. The Developer cannot access it. You can turn off iCloud Sync and automatic backups at any time in the App's Settings tab.

6. Advertising

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display ads and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage consent. The App shows banner ads, interstitial ads (full-screen ads shown, for example, when you save a formation) and rewarded ads (video ads that unlock a locked sample kit when watched to the end). The App requests non-personalized ads, does not ask for permission through App Tracking Transparency, and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

Google may collect the following information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and improve quality:

  • Approximate location inferred from an IP address
  • Device information, such as device type and OS version
  • Ads shown, and interactions with ads and the App
  • Crash logs, performance data and other diagnostic information

Whether this information is collected, and to what extent, depends on your region, device settings and consent choices.

7. Managing consent

In regions where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, a Google UMP consent form is shown when the App starts. You can also manage advertising-related settings in Privacy & Security in your device's Settings app.

8. Analytics and diagnostics

The App does not use any analytics tools of the Developer's own. If you have turned on “Share With App Developers” in your device settings, the Developer may receive crash logs and other diagnostic information from Apple in a form that does not identify you. This information is used only to fix problems in the App.

9. Retention and deletion

Data on your device remains until you delete it in the App or delete the App. Data may reappear if you restore a device backup or one of the App's automatic backups.

Synced data and automatic backups stored in iCloud Drive may remain after you delete the App. To remove them, use the “XI Board” folder in the Files app, or go to Settings > [your name] > iCloud on your device. Information collected by Google is retained and deleted according to Google's policies.

10. Children

The App does not collect users' ages. Anyone who needs parental supervision should use the App under the supervision of a parent or guardian.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when laws, App features or third-party services change. Important changes will be announced on this page or in the App.
